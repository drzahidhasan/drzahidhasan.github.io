About

I hold a PhD in Health Economics from the University of Leeds, and a Bachelor's and Master's in Health Economics from the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh. Since 2016, I have worked on multidisciplinary health systems research in Bangladesh.

Primary healthcare

Cost-effectiveness and budget impact of interventions.
